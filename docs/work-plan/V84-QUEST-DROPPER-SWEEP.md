# Does the dropper live where the quest sends you?

Follow-up to the 22529 Refreshing Stump Sap miss. Generated by
`tools/playthrough/questdroppersweep.py`; the machine-readable table is
`docs/work-plan/V84-QUEST-DROPPER-SWEEP.tsv`.

```
python tools/playthrough/questdroppersweep.py
```

## Why the original 22529 reasoning was wrong

The Stump Sap row was authored by reading `#o0130100#` out of the quest's own text, and then
defended by pointing back at that same token. That is circular. **Client WZ files never contained
drop tables** — Nexon kept drops server-side — so *no drop row in this tree is recovered v84 data*.
Every one is somebody's reading of something else, and a token cannot corroborate a row derived
from the token.

Three things in the same data contradicted it and were not weighed: `QuestInfo/22529/0` stages the
quest in Deep Valley I/II/III, which hold zero mob 130100; the quest is level 22 and mob 130100 is
level 4; and the instruction is *"Defeat the `#r`Stumps`#k`"* — a bare plural naming the family,
not the id. The owner is right, and the three Deep Valley variants are being added by changeSet 170.

## The rule this sweep runs under

> "It's better to take the text of the entire quest, the pre-accept and the accept quest text, as
> the true hints." — the owner

**The checks are triage. The quest's own whole text is the authority.** Each heuristic has honest
exceptions: a quest may legitimately be *given* somewhere other than where it is *done*; a
high-level quest may legitimately want a low-level drop; a plural may legitimately mean the one id.
So every hit below was read in full — `QuestInfo/0`, `/1`, `/2`, every other string node,
`Say.img/<id>` both branches, and the `String.wz` name of every mob, item, npc and map the text
references — before a verdict was written.

## Scope

- The 198 v84-added quests already enumerated in `V84-QUEST-SWEEP.md`.
- **Only drop rows this project authored.** `151-global-drop-data.sql` and `152-drop-data.sql` are
  the upstream HeavenMS seed, a different lineage, and are out of scope. Ours are 153, 154, 155,
  156, 159, 160, 168 and 170.
- 46 quest/item pairs carry a drop row at all; 19 of them carry one of ours.

## What the sweep learned, encoded so it does not re-fire

- **Portal scripts are routes.** `tm=999999999` is not a dead end; the destination is in
  `scripts/portal/<name>.js`. The neighbour graph now follows `warp(<mapid>)` out of them.
- **`NAME_LINK` is evidence, never a verdict.** `String.wz` item names and descriptions are client
  data and are not derived from any drop row, so "Lycanthrope Leather ← Lycanthrope" corroborates
  independently. Its limit is family granularity: *"Refreshing Stump Sap"* matches "Stump", "Axe
  Stump" and "Ghost Stump" equally, which is precisely how 22529 got through. The flag is recorded
  and never auto-clears a row.
- **`ALSO_UPSTREAM_SOURCED`** marks items that upstream already drops. Our row there is additive and
  cannot be the reason a quest is uncompletable.
- **Ordinary loot is skipped** (>20 droppers): "is the dropper where the quest is" is meaningless
  for an Orange Potion.

## Columns

`quest_id · quest_name · lvmin · item · droppers (ours, else all) · seed_changesets ·
quest_map_tokens · dropper_on_quest_map · dropper_levels · heuristic_verdict · flags ·
reviewed_verdict · evidence`

`heuristic_verdict` is triage output. `reviewed_verdict` is the conclusion after reading the whole
quest, and is the only column to act on.
